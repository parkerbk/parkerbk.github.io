Coach's Clipboard Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Coach's Clipboard does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. The developer has no servers and never sees your plays, teams, or rosters.

What stays on your devices

  • Plays, drawings, teams, and rosters are stored on your device.
  • iCloud sync: if you're signed in to iCloud, your plays and teams sync between your devices through your private iCloud account. Apple stores this data under its privacy policy; the developer cannot access it.
  • Sharing: videos, PDFs, and play files go only where you send them, using the system share sheet.

Permissions

  • Microphone: asked only when you choose Record Voiceover, to record your narration for a play video. The recording stays on your device and goes only into the video you export.
  • Photos (add only): asked only when you choose Save Video, to save an exported play video to your photo library. The app cannot see your photos.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple. The developer receives no payment details, name, or email address from a purchase.

No tracking

Coach's Clipboard contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking code.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. Player names you enter for a roster stay on your devices and in your iCloud account.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Coach's Clipboard

Draw, record, and play back plays for basketball, football, soccer, and baseball, on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Support

Questions, bug reports, or feature requests: email support@scrubbedcode.com.

Subscriptions

Quick Start is free: draw and record a play, play it back, and export it as a video, a PDF, or a play file. Coach's Clipboard Pro adds teams with rosters, team colors, and playbooks, including playbook PDFs. Pro is $1.99 a month, $4.99 for three months (one season), or $14.99 a year (US prices). To change or cancel, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, or tap the star in the app's sidebar and choose Manage. Teams made while subscribed stay saved if a subscription ends and come back when you subscribe again.

Spiral-bound Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Spiral-bound does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. The developer has no servers and never sees your notes, recordings, transcripts, or summaries.

What stays on your devices

  • Notes, handwriting, recordings, transcripts, and summaries are stored on your devices. Speech is transcribed on the device with Apple's speech recognition, and summaries are written by Apple's on-device model.
  • Speaker detection, if you turn it on, runs on the device. Turning it on downloads the speaker models once from Hugging Face; that download sends none of your data, though, like any download, Hugging Face sees your device's IP address.
  • Imported recordings are copied into the app and processed on the device, like recordings made in the app.
  • Apple Watch recordings go only to your paired iPhone.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud and leave iCloud Sync on, your meetings sync between your devices through your private iCloud account. Apple stores this data under its privacy policy; the developer cannot access it. You can turn sync off in the app's Settings.

Sharing you choose

Your notes leave the app only when you send them somewhere yourself: Copy to Notes, exporting a PDF, sharing a recording, or adding action items to Reminders. Where they go after that is up to you and that app.

Purchases

Spiral-bound Unlimited is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment; the developer receives no payment details or personal information, and the app checks the purchase with Apple's StoreKit on your device.

Permissions

The app asks for these only when you use the related feature, and you can turn them off in Settings at any time:

  • Microphone (and, on Mac, the audio of a call you choose): to record the meeting.
  • Speech recognition: to transcribe recordings on the device.
  • Camera: to scan documents into a meeting.
  • Contacts: to show the contact for an @name in your notes.
  • Reminders: to add the action items you approve to Reminders.

Pictures you add come through the system photo picker, which shares only the pictures you pick and needs no permission.

No tracking

Spiral-bound contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking code.

Recording others

Laws on recording conversations vary. You are responsible for getting any consent required where you live before recording a meeting or call.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

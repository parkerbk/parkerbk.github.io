Spiral-bound Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Spiral-bound does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. The developer has no servers and never sees your notes, recordings, or transcripts.

What stays on your devices

  • Notes, drawings, recordings, and transcripts are stored on your device. Speech is transcribed on the device using Apple's speech recognition, and summaries use Apple's on-device models.
  • iCloud sync: if you're signed in to iCloud, your notes sync between your devices through your private iCloud account. Apple stores this data under its privacy policy; the developer cannot access it.

Permissions

The app asks for these only when you use the related feature, and you can turn them off in Settings at any time:

  • Microphone and, on Mac, system audio: to record the meeting or call you choose.
  • Speech recognition: to transcribe recordings on the device.
  • Camera: to scan documents into a note.
  • Photos: to add the pictures you pick to a note.
  • Reminders: to add action items you approve to Reminders.

No tracking

Spiral-bound contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking code.

Recording others

Laws on recording conversations vary. You are responsible for getting any consent required where you live before recording a meeting or call.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

National Pastime Scorebook Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

National Pastime Scorebook does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. The developer has no servers and never sees your teams, players, games, or music.

What stays on your devices

  • Teams, players, lineups, games, scores, stats, photos, and walk-up clips are stored on your device, including audio files you import.
  • iCloud sync: if you're signed in to iCloud, this data syncs between your devices through your private iCloud account. Apple stores it under its privacy policy; the developer cannot access it.

Apple Music

If you allow access, the app searches the Apple Music catalog and plays the songs you pick. Searches and playback go to Apple, not to the developer. You can turn access off in Settings at any time.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple. The developer receives no payment details, name, or email address from a purchase.

No tracking

The app has no accounts and contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking code.

Children

Coaches often enter the names of players who are children. That information stays on the coach's devices and in the coach's private iCloud account. The developer does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete a player, game, or team in the app to remove it from your devices and iCloud. Deleting the app removes the copy on that device. To remove what's stored in iCloud, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud and Manage Storage.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

National Pastime Scorebook

Lineups, walk-up music, and a full scorebook for baseball and softball coaches, on iPhone and iPad.

Support

Questions, bug reports, or feature requests: email support@scrubbedcode.com.

Subscriptions

Teams, rosters, lineups, walk-up music, and game mode are free. Scoring and Stats are optional subscriptions, or get both with All Access. To change or cancel, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, or tap the star on the app's Teams screen and choose Manage.

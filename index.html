Common questions

  • No summary? Summaries need Apple Intelligence turned on in the Settings app, on a device that supports it.
  • No transcript? The first time, your device may need to download the speech model for your language; keep Spiral-bound open on Wi-Fi and it continues.
  • Meetings not on your other devices? Check that each device is signed in to the same iCloud account and that iCloud Sync is on in the app's Settings.
  • A recording from another phone or a voice recorder? Hold + in the meeting list (File > Import Recording on the Mac) and choose the audio file.
